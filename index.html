The problem

Customers who move country can’t update their country of residence in the app. Some likely leave. Those who stay have to go through Support, often more than once.

Five hypotheses

#HypothesisFirst test
H1Customers don’t realise the field is locked on purpose. They think the app is broken.Interview recent movers; read chat transcripts
H2Emailing documents lowers completion. Customers struggle to find the right email address, and trust is low when sending personal documents to a generic support address.Funnel: documents requested to documents received; ask movers how they found the address
H3Customers accept a slower check if the app shows clear status.Prototype test: status shown or not
H4Self-serve exists in some countries, but customers can’t tell whether it exists in theirs.Entry-point analytics on the profile screen
H5After the change, parts of the app still follow the old country, like referral offers in yen. Customers can’t tell if the change is finished or still in progress, or whether they can use the app. So they go back to Support.Repeat contacts after a change; contacts that mention the old currency or ask if the change is done

The solution, in phases

If I had an engineering team for three months, I’d fix the clarity problem first, then the process. Timings are my estimates.

P0 · about a week

Add a clear link

Make the lock tappable. It reveals a “Moved recently?” link to the right help article.

P1

In-app flow

New address, upload proof, see status. Checks run in the background.

P2

Switch the whole app

Currency, display and content follow the new country.

P3

Assistant

Guides customers and hands off to a person. Then more countries.

Happy and unhappy paths are walked through in the customer journey (switch to “With the fix”).

How I’d check it works

  • Replay past chats offline first
  • Block any reply that states a verification result
  • Track hand-offs and second contacts

What to measure

First test

  • Test: today’s screen against a screen with the tappable lock and link
  • Success: fewer region-change contacts per 1,000 customers who open the screen
  • Watch: failed verifications, compliance flags, repeat contacts
  • Catch: few customers move each week, so check volumes before setting a sample size

Open questions

  • Which countries have the in-app option, and which don’t?
  • What does the app change today when the country changes, and what still needs Support?
  • Why did the app keep showing yen? Is an update missing, or does it depend on balances held in the old currency?
  • One Wise help page says it can’t accept documents by email. The greyed-out route says to contact Wise with a document. Which is it?
  • Do customers really get a result within the 2 days Wise states?
  • Is there a secure upload channel already used for identity checks that this flow could reuse?
